### Changed

- **Breaking (Store port):** `Store.commit` takes its deletes as refs, `deletes: ReadonlyArray<ChangedRecord>` (`{ id, tagName, parentId }`, the parent the record had) instead of ids. A store deletes by `id` and keeps the stored record as the history image: the ref's tag and parent only tell the change. A custom store reads `ref.id`; code calling `store.commit` passes refs.
- **Breaking (Store port):** `Store.undo` and `Store.redo` answer `HistoryStep | false` instead of `void`: `{ changes }` of what the step applied (an undo the commit's inverse, a redo the commit), `false` when there was nothing to step (nothing written, nothing told). A custom store builds the answer with `describeHistoryStep({ images, direction })` over its history entry's images (exported, with `describeCommitChanges`, `HistoryStep`, `HistoryImages` and `HistoryStepDirection`); a store that announces tells a commit with `describeCommitChanges` over its arguments and an undo or a redo with its answer's changes.
- A commit, an undo and a redo reach every connection with their exact `changes` (they were `{ unknown: true }` for undo, redo and announced commits): on `inMemory`, `local` (IndexedDB) and `sqlite` alike, past 5,000 refs `{ unknown: true }`. A bulk write stays `{ unknown: true }`.
- `project.undo()` and `project.redo()` with nothing to step post nothing and signal no state change, on every store (an IndexedDB project posted a `commit` before).
- `STORAGE_KINDS_VERSION` is 11, the SQLite hub protocol 11 and the engine wire protocol 7: a copy of the library at an older version finds no storage kind registered by this one (`PROJECT_STORAGE_NOT_REGISTERED`), and a realm or engine of another version is refused as before.
