### Added

- `@dialecte/core/test` proves a read cache against the uncached path: `runReadModelCases` runs cases on every store given, each with a writer and a reader project of one name, typed by the dialecte's config. The reader's store sits behind a gate (`gateStore`: hold a call before the store, hold the store's answer until after a change, hold the store's notices, count calls), the realm's BroadcastChannel is made lossy (`installLossyChannel`: drop, hold or reorder a message) and a store is held directly (`openRawStore`, `writeAround`: a change no project makes or tells). `waitForChange`, `answerUncached`, `createVersionTrail`, `expectSnapshot` (an answer of the session, never older than one it gave before) and `expectExposed` state what a cached answer may be. Core pins its own read caches with it (the read scope of one verb call, the transaction read cache) on the in-memory, IndexedDB and SQLite stores: a heard change, an answer read before a change coming back after it was heard, a commit too large to tell its changes, and the changes a reader is not told yet (a lost message, a write around the projects, a notice not folded).
