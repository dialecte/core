### Changed

- The `dialecte` skill gains `references/ask-ladder.md`: an agent's own question about a dialecte goes through `dialecte ask` before any person - the facts recorded before, the definition, the catalogues and api index, then the standard's documents for readers. It follows the ask's `next` line by exit code: `ask pack` and independent readers whose reports `ask verdict` checks, the adjudicator at high effort, and a person last, whose reply `ask answer` (or `lot answer`) records so it is not asked twice. An answer from outside the documents stays unchecked until a person confirms it.
- The `dialecte-lot` skill's standard check runs the cross-check of a decision through `ask --decision --lot`, `ask pack`, the readers' reports and `ask verdict`, and keeps the result next to the plan as well as in the facts ledger; the ask writes the lot's `standard` note itself. The manual cross-check stays for a cli without `ask`. Station 0's discovery list starts with `dialecte ask`.
